# Session 10 interview snapshot: P2

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. Wayfinder is not a real product, and all participants, venues, transit routes and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P2` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Trip rhythm: 4 to 5 long weekends (Friday to Monday) a year across European cities with their partner, typical weekend budget around `$180` to `$220` per person excluding flights and hotel
- Setup: saved map pins, travel blog bookmarks and an Apple Notes checklist added after last month's trip
- Format: 30-minute video call (16 minutes story interview, 14 minutes static probe test on mobile)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 16:00)

- **Opening general claim (contradicted by last month's Lisbon story):** At minute 2, `P2` leaned back and said: "Honestly, we never plan weekends, we just wing it. Over-planning ruins the magic of a city break. We drop our bags, walk out of the guesthouse and see what looks good."
- **Asked: walk me through your most recent long weekend, specifically the Sunday or Monday.**
  - Paused, laughed sheepishly, and reversed the opening claim: "Okay, since you asked about Monday specifically, last month in Lisbon was a small disaster."
  - **What actually happened in Lisbon:** On Monday morning it poured with heavy rain. `P2` and their partner walked 35 minutes uphill in wet trainers to a tile museum they had bookmarked from a three-year-old blog post, assuming museums open at `10:00` every day. Arrived soaked to find padlocked gates (`Closed Mondays`).
  - Quote: "We stood under a bakery awning in sideways rain for 55 minutes arguing on a wet street corner, scrolling ten ad-heavy blog posts on 4G trying to find something indoor nearby. The aquarium timed slots were sold out until 16:30, a botanical conservatory was also shut on Mondays, and we wasted $26.00 on two surge-priced rideshares zigzagging across town before giving up and sitting in a shopping-centre food court."
- **Current workaround (Apple Notes checklist):** Opened phone (did not share screen) and read out the note they created on the flight home from Lisbon: `Weekend Sanity Check`. Before every trip they now manually look up each saved pin's Monday/Tuesday closure day, last-entry cutoff and one indoor rainy-day backup within 15 minutes.
  - Quote: "So yeah, I say we wing it, and then I spend Tuesday night copying opening hours off museum websites into Apple Notes so we never have the wet street-corner argument again."
- **Tangent (minutes 13 to 16, ~3 minutes):** Mentioned it would be cute if an app stitched phone photos into a 30-second weekend highlight video for family on Monday night.

## Part 2: Static probe test (16:00 to 30:00)

Shown the Level 2 static HTML discovery probe `Day Plan & Budget Split` on mobile (`Per-person cap: $180.00`; 3 booked stops totalling `$94.50`: `Harbour Ferry $18.50`, `Tile Museum $22.00`, `Sunset Kayak $54.00`; `$85.50` with muted grey caption `Left after booked stops (meals and local transit not included)`; `16:00 ferry transfer clash` warning; no booking or hold CTA on screen).

- **Task ("Tell me what stands out on Saturday's plan and whether you would change anything"):**
  - Read the `16:00` clash warning first: "That amber block between Tile Museum and Sunset Kayak is great. Map pins never warn you that 42 minutes on the coastal tram makes a 16:00 kayak check-in impossible."
  - Immediately asked about weather and opening hours: "If Saturday afternoon turns into heavy rain and Sunset Kayak cancels, or if we move Tile Museum to Monday, does this screen actually know Tile Museum is closed on Mondays?"
- **Comprehension check ("What does `$85.50` mean for the rest of your weekend spend?"):**
  - Squinted at the top card for six seconds before answering: "At first I read '$85.50' as what I have left after everything, then I caught the little grey bracket saying `(meals and local transit not included)`. Please don't hide that in tiny grey text. Two casual dinners and coffees in Lisbon easily hit $70.00 per person, so $85.50 before meals is actually pretty tight, not spacious."
