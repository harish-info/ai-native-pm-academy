Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. Wayfinder is not a real product, and all participants, venues, transit routes and figures are invented.

- Participant: `P5` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 17:00)

- **Step-free transit reality versus standard map estimates:**
  - Quote: "Standard map apps tell you two stops are 14 minutes apart because they assume you bound down two flights of metro stairs and jog across a cobbled overpass. With a stroller or someone who needs lifts, one out-of-service station lift turns a 14-minute transfer into a 32-minute detour in the drizzle."
- **Asked: walk me through the last specific weekend trip where hours or transit caught you out.**
  - Story from a bank-holiday Monday weekend in Brussels last month (`$175.00` per-adult activity budget):
  - Checked a popular travel roundup that listed a comic-art museum as `"Open daily 10:00 to 18:00"`. Took two trams and a detour around a closed station lift (`28 minutes` slower than planned), arriving at `15:20` on the bank-holiday Monday.
  - Quote: "The security guard pointed at a tiny paper sign saying bank-holiday Mondays close last entry at 15:00 instead of 17:00. Our toddler was melting down, it started raining, we paid $28.00 for a last-minute taxi back to the hotel and ate $19.00 station sandwiches for dinner."
- **Current workaround:** Before committing to any afternoon stop, opens three separate tabs on their phone: the venue's own website footer for holiday/Monday hours, the city transit operator's step-free accessibility page, and a shared note tracking booked ticket totals separately from meals.
- **Tangent (minutes 14 to 17, ~3 minutes):** Mentioned wishing train operators across Europe standardised stroller-space reservation icons on intercity booking confirmations.

## Part 2: Static probe test (17:00 to 30:00)

Shown the Level 2 static HTML discovery probe `Day Plan & Budget Split` on mobile (`Per-person cap: $180.00`; 3 booked stops totalling `$94.50`: `Harbour Ferry $18.50`, `Tile Museum $22.00`, `Sunset Kayak $54.00`; `$85.50` with small muted grey caption `Left after booked stops (meals and local transit not included)`; `16:00 ferry transfer clash` warning; no booking or hold CTA on screen).

- **Task ("Tell me whether Saturday works for your trip and what questions you have before trusting it"):**
  - Spotted the `16:00` clash warning right away, then **tapped the `Tile Museum $22.00` row three times** expecting an expandable detail drawer.
  - Quote: "I kept tapping Tile Museum because two things make or break whether I trust this screen. First, are those opening hours verified against official Monday and bank-holiday schedules, and how recently were they checked? Second, does that '42 min by tram' buffer assume stairs at the interchange, or is it step-free for a stroller?"
- **Comprehension check ("How do you read the `$85.50` figure at the top?"):**
  - Read the `$85.50` math accurately after spotting the caption (`$180.00 - $94.50 = $85.50`), and echoed the contrast issue:
  - Quote: "I caught the grey line saying `(meals and local transit not included)` because I already track tickets separately in my own note, though on a bright train platform with a wriggling toddler that pale grey font is invisible. Put 'Left after booked stops (meals and local transit not included)' in dark readable text right next to `$85.50` so nobody confuses it with an all-in remainder."
