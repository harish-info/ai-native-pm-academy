# Session 10 interview snapshot: P4

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. Wayfinder is not a real product, and all participants, venues, transit routes and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P4` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Trip rhythm: default organiser for 2 to 3 annual weekend trips with a group of 4 friends, typical per-person weekend cap `$180` to `$200`
- Setup: 4-person WhatsApp group chat full of social-media screenshots, a manual 6-column Google Sheet built on a laptop, and a bill-splitting app after the trip
- Format: 30-minute video call (18 minutes story interview, 12 minutes static probe test on mobile)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 18:00)

- **Group-coordination bottleneck (screenshots buried in chat):**
  - Quote: "Every group trip starts the exact same way. Four friends drop forty screenshots of bakeries, rooftop bars and canal boats into WhatsApp over three weeks with 'ooh let's do this!!' Nobody types an address, nobody checks which side of the city anything is on, and I become the unpaid travel agent."
- **Asked: walk me through what happened on your group's last weekend trip.**
  - Story from last month's 4-friend weekend in Amsterdam (`$190.00` agreed per-person cap):
  - **Buried allergy constraint:** Ten days before the trip, one friend wrote in WhatsApp: `"Reminder, severe tree-nut allergy, please avoid almond/hazelnut pastry kitchens."` Thirty-four messages and twelve screenshots later, someone else posted a Saturday afternoon Dutch pastry workshop (`$38.00` per person, non-refundable). `P4` booked four spots at midnight without scrolling back up. At the door, the host warned almond paste was used on every bench; the friend had to sit in a cafe next door alone and lost `$38.00`.
  - **Neighbourhood transit scramble:** Nobody checked transit between a morning market in Noord (requiring a ferry plus metro) and a `13:00` museum slot in Oud-Zuid (`38 minutes` door-to-door). They ran the final 800 metres and arrived flustered with three minutes to spare.
  - **Budget mismatch inside the group:** Two friends thought `$190.00` meant _tickets only_, while the other two thought `$190.00` was _all-in including dinners and trams_, sparking an awkward Sunday evening chat when total spend hit `$275.00` each.
- **Current workaround:** Spends two hours on a Wednesday night copying screenshots out of WhatsApp into a 6-column laptop Google Sheet (`Stop`, `Neighbourhood`, `Cost`, `Hours`, `Transit to next`, `Allergy/Notes`).
  - Quote: "When I tried a popular trip-planner app in January, it asked me to type every venue name by hand on a blank screen. All our ideas were sitting in twelve WhatsApp screenshots, so I left the trip empty and closed the app."
- **Tangent (minutes 15 to 18, ~3 minutes):** Joked about wanting an automated "late friend alarm" that rings the hotel room of whichever friend takes longest drying their hair before brunch.

## Part 2: Static probe test (18:00 to 30:00)

Shown the Level 2 static HTML discovery probe `Day Plan & Budget Split` on mobile (`Per-person cap: $180.00`; 3 booked stops totalling `$94.50`: `Harbour Ferry $18.50`, `Tile Museum $22.00`, `Sunset Kayak $54.00`; `$85.50` with small muted grey caption `Left after booked stops (meals and local transit not included)`; `16:00 ferry transfer clash` warning; no booking or hold CTA on screen).

- **Task ("Imagine sharing this Saturday plan with your 4-person group. What works and what causes confusion?"):**
  - Pointed straight at the `16:00 ferry transfer clash` banner: "That 42-minute door-to-door tram check is gold. Nobody in a group chat ever checks travel time between neighbourhoods until you're already late."
  - **Misread `$85.50` at first glance, then caught the group trap:** Initially said, "And $85.50 per person left for Sunday is super clear... wait. Does $94.50 include Saturday lunch and tram passes, or is it literally just the three tickets?" Checked `18.50 + 22.00 + 54.00 = 94.50` and groaned: "Oh, see, that tiny grey line underneath is the exact argument my group had in Amsterdam! If I screenshot this screen into WhatsApp and it says '$85.50' in huge bold numbers, two friends will read it as all-in remaining budget. Never label it 'remaining' on its own, make the main label say 'Left after booked stops (meals and local transit not included)' in full contrast."
  - Asked before finishing: "Also, where do group constraints like `tree-nut allergy` sit on this plan so if Sunset Kayak rains out and we swap in a food tour, it doesn't suggest an almond bakery?"
