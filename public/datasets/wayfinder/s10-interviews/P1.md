# Session 10 interview snapshot: P1

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. Wayfinder is not a real product, and all participants, venues, transit routes and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P1` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded at start for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Trip rhythm: plans 3 to 4 Friday-to-Sunday city breaks a year with a partner or one close friend, usually aiming for `$170` to `$200` per person on weekend activities and local transport
- Setup: shared Google Doc for rough day ideas, airline app, mobile maps and a shared notes list for splitting ticket costs
- Format: 30-minute video call (17 minutes story interview, 13 minutes static probe test on mobile)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 17:00)

- **Context and planning habit:** Usually books flights six weeks out, drops five or six museum and boat-tour links into a Google Doc, and pre-pays two timed tickets on the Wednesday before flying so Saturday has "anchors".
- **Asked: walk me through the last specific weekend trip where the plan or budget went sideways.**
  - Story from last month in Porto: `P1` pre-booked `River Tram Museum` (`$19.00` per person at `14:00`) and a `Vila Nova Cellar Tour` (`$34.00` per person at `15:30` across the river).
  - Quote: "In the Google Doc, 14:00 and 15:30 looked totally relaxed, ninety minutes apart. Nobody checked that the museum takes an hour, walking down to the bridge takes twenty minutes, and the tram queue across the river was another twenty-five. We sprinted up the hill at 15:52 sweating through our coats, missed our slot, and lost $68.00 for the two tickets."
  - **Budget surprise on the same weekend:** They had agreed on `$200.00` per person for the weekend, saw `$92.00` of pre-booked tickets in the Google Doc on Friday morning, and felt flush ("We thought we had over $100 each of fun money"). By Sunday night they had spent `$262.00` each (`$62.00` over cap) because nobody counted two dinners (`$96.00`), coffees and pastries (`$28.00`) and airport rail plus metro taps (`$46.00`).
- **Current workaround:** Now adds manual `[+35 min travel?]` guesses in brackets between lines in the Google Doc, though `P1` admitted they only check one map lookup before giving up.
- **Tangent (minutes 12 to 16, ~4 minutes, out-of-scope distraction):** Pivoted enthusiastically to airfare hacking and loyalty points:
  - Quote: "Honestly, the biggest win for me would be if Wayfinder scraped budget airline price drops at 3am and pinged me when 14,000 airline miles beat paying $140.00 cash for the Friday flight, or auto-tracked credit-card lounge passes." _(Observer note: `P1` already uses two dedicated flight-alert websites for flights; none of last month's weekend friction came from airfare.)_

## Part 2: Static probe test (17:00 to 30:00)

Shown the Level 2 static HTML discovery probe `Day Plan & Budget Split` on mobile (Saturday city-break view; `Per-person cap: $180.00`; 3 booked stops totalling `$94.50`: `10:30 Harbour Ferry $18.50`, `13:30 Tile Museum $22.00`, `16:15 Sunset Kayak $54.00` at Belém Dock; headline `$85.50` with a small muted grey caption `Left after booked stops (meals and local transit not included)`; amber warning banner between stop 2 and stop 3 reading `16:00 ferry transfer clash: Tile Museum to Belém Dock takes 42 min door-to-door by tram; leaving at 15:40 misses the 16:00 kayak check-in`. As a Level 2 static probe, there is no booking or reservation-hold button on screen).

- **Task ("Walk me through how Saturday looks, whether the timing works, and where your budget stands"):**
  - Pointed immediately at the `16:00 ferry transfer clash` banner: "Oh, thank goodness, that 42-minute door-to-door warning is the exact Porto bridge mistake that cost us $68.00 last month. Seeing 'leaving at 15:40 misses the 16:00 check-in' tells me right away I have to move Tile Museum earlier."
- **Comprehension check ("What does `$85.50` cover for the rest of the trip?"):**
  - **Misread `$85.50` as all-in remaining:** Answered briskly, "We're in great shape, $85.50 left per person for Sunday extras, and Saturday dinner and our metro day passes are already bundled into the $94.50, right?"
  - Prompted to check the three stop prices (`$18.50 + $22.00 + $54.00`): `P1` added them aloud, paused five seconds, then zoomed in on the muted grey line underneath `$85.50`.
  - Quote: "Wait. $18.50 plus $22.00 is $40.50, plus $54.00 is $94.50. Subtract that from $180.00 and you get $85.50! There isn't a single dollar in there for dinner, lunch or the tram! I completely skimmed over that tiny grey caption. If you just show a big '$85.50 left' at the top, my brain reads 'all-in spending money after food', and I'll blow the budget by $60.00 again. Make the label itself say 'Left after booked stops (meals and local transit not included)'."
