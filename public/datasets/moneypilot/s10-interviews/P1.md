# Session 10 interview snapshot: P1

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. MoneyPilot is not a real product, and all participants, accounts, merchants and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P1` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded at start for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Pay schedule: monthly on the 25th (previous Friday if the 25th falls on a weekend)
- Setup: one main current account for salary, `AvtaleGiro` / `eFaktura` bills and daily card spending, plus a separate "Holiday fund" instant-access savings account in the same banking app
- Format: 30-minute video call (17 minutes story interview, 13 minutes prototype test on mobile)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 17:00)

- **Context and monthly rhythm:** Paid on the 25th. Rent and Eastmere Municipal Fees leave on the 1st and 3rd, so the first week after payday feels comfortable ("On the 26th I feel weirdly rich for about four days"). The squeeze starts around the 17th or 18th when smaller `AvtaleGiro` bills are still scattered across the last week.
- **Current workaround (Apple Notes checklist):** Opened phone (did not share screen) and read out the title of an Apple Note called `Bills 2026`. It lists nine recurring payments with rough day numbers typed by hand, for example `Broadband 15th - 340 kr`, `Electric 14th - 820 kr`, `Gym 20th - 300 kr`, `Car ins 22nd - 620 kr`.
  - Quote: "Around the 18th or 19th I sit on the sofa flipping between Apple Notes and my bank statement, ticking little circles to work out who has already taken their money. If the 15th was a Sunday, Fenwick Broadband doesn't show up until Tuesday the 17th, so I am scrolling back three weeks trying to spot whether it went out or not."
- **Asked: walk me through the last specific time a bill caught you off guard before payday.**
  - Story from mid-February: Northlight Electricity ran a winter usage adjustment and raised `P1`'s monthly `AvtaleGiro` from `820.00 kr` to `1,340.00 kr` on 14 February (`520.00 kr` higher).
  - Quote: "They did email me at the end of January, I checked afterwards and it was sitting in my archive, so technically it was my fault. But my Apple Note still said 820 kr. By Friday the 20th my balance was 1,400 kr and I genuinely couldn't work out why it was 500 kr lower than my head maths."
  - **What `P1` actually did next:** Transferred money out of the Holiday fund three separate times between 20 February and 24 February (`300.00 kr` on Friday, `200.00 kr` on Sunday, `250.00 kr` on Monday).
  - Quote: "I raided my Holiday fund three times in four days, 300 kr then 200 kr then 250 kr, because I couldn't tell if 1,400 kr just had to cover groceries and cat food until Wednesday or if Kestrel Car Insurance was still waiting to come out."
- **Tangent (minutes 12 to 15, ~3 minutes):** Went off-topic about switching current accounts last October to claim a `1,750 kr` bank switching offer, forgetting to update a debit card with Pulse Gym and getting a reminder SMS, then complained about supermarket loyalty app pricing ("48.00 kr for oat milk if you forget to scan the member app is daylight robbery"). Ended the tangent with an off-the-cuff wish: "Honestly what I would love is taking a photo of my supermarket receipt so the app splits cat food and cleaning stuff away from our actual food budget."

## Part 2: Prototype test (17:00 to 30:00)

Shown the mobile prototype screen `Safe-to-spend and upcoming bills` (test date `18 March 2026`, payday `25 March`; current balance `4,100.00 kr`; 4 scheduled bills totalling `4,550.00 kr`: Eastmere Municipal Fees `1,520.00 kr` due in 2 days on 20 March, Northlight Electricity `1,180.00 kr` due in 3 days on 21 March, Fenwick Broadband `350.00 kr` due in 5 days on 23 March, Halden Finance Car Loan `1,500.00 kr` due in 6 days on 24 March; headline `Safe before payday (24 March): -450.00 kr`; bottom button `Prepare 500 kr transfer from Holiday fund`).

- **Task ("Tell me whether you are okay until payday, and why"):**
  - Found `-450.00 kr` within four seconds and read out all four bills in date order without hesitation.
  - Pointed at `"due in 2 days"` beside `20 March`: "Having both 'due in 2 days' and the date is miles better than my Notes list, because my brain thinks in days left, not calendar dates."
- **Comprehension check ("What is included in `-450.00 kr`, and what happens if you tap `Prepare 500 kr transfer`?"):**
  - **Misread everyday spending (failed first part):** Said confidently, "So I'm 450 kr short overall, which means if I move 500 kr from the Holiday fund I'll land on plus 50 kr on the 24th, food and bus tickets included, tight but fine."
  - When prompted to check how `-450.00 kr` was calculated from `4,100.00 kr` and `4,550.00 kr`, `P1` stopped for six seconds, scrolled up and down twice looking for a groceries line, then blinked: "Wait. 4,100 kr minus 4,550 kr is already minus 450 kr. There is zero in there for food shopping! That tiny grey line underneath says 'Scheduled bills and expected income', I completely skipped past it. If I transferred 500 kr and saw '+50.00 kr safe before payday', I would think my usual weekly shop was already factored in and go spend 650 kr at the supermarket."
  - **Button expectation (passed second part with wording note):** "Prepare sounds like it sets up a draft transfer in the drawer and then asks me to confirm it on the next screen, though 'Prepare' is slightly odd bank wording, usually it says 'Review transfer' or 'Draft transfer'."
