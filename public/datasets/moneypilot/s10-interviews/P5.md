# Session 10 interview snapshot: P5

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. MoneyPilot is not a real product, and all participants, accounts, merchants and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P5` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Pay schedule: monthly on the 25th
- Setup: main household current account used for salary, kindergarten fees, Halden Finance Car Loan, household utilities and family food shopping, plus one savings account
- Format: 30-minute video call (17 minutes story interview, 13 minutes prototype test on mobile; toddler audible in background during final 5 minutes)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 17:00)

- **Current workaround (laptop Google Sheet abandoned mid-month):** Built a colour-coded Google Sheet (`2026 Family Cashflow`) on a laptop in January listing 14 monthly `AvtaleGiro`, `eFaktura` and card bills. Checks and updates it on the 1st of the month after bedtime, then never opens it between the 10th and the 25th.
  - Quote: "A spreadsheet looks brilliant at 9pm on the 1st on a laptop. Nobody opens Google Sheets one-handed in the supermarket aisle on the 21st with a two-year-old kicking the trolley. You open the bank app, see 2,100 kr, and do panicked guesswork."
- **"The squinting week" (18th to 24th of the month):**
  - Quote: "My partner and I literally call the 18th to the 24th 'the squinting week'. Even when we technically have 900 kr spare after the remaining bills, we cannot tell at a glance whether Halden Finance Car Loan (1,850 kr) or Brookline Water has left yet without scrolling forty card transactions. Last month on the 22nd I put the bulk box of nappies back on the shelf until payday morning just because I wasn't sure what was still queued."
- **Asked: walk me through the last specific time a bill surprised you before payday.**
  - Story from `20 February`: Tellmore Mobile normally leaves at `220.00 kr` on the 20th (`220 kr` was hardcoded in the Google Sheet six months ago). During a short family visit abroad in January, `P5` answered two long roaming calls out of bundle, pushing the `20 February` `AvtaleGiro` to `694.00 kr` (`474.00 kr` extra).
  - Quote: "I never open mobile invoice PDFs because it is 220 kr eleven months a year. On Friday the 20th it took 694.00 kr, left us with 190 kr for four days of food shopping, and I had to pull 750 kr out of our rainy-day savings account at the checkout."
- **Tangent (minutes 13 to 17, ~4 minutes):** Spent three minutes venting about the municipal kindergarten billing portal sending separate meal-fee adjustments mid-semester with confusing PDF attachments, then mentioned: "One day it would be nice if my partner could see a simple read-only widget of which household bills haven't gone out yet without us sharing logins to my personal bank account."

## Part 2: Prototype test (17:00 to 30:00)

Shown the mobile prototype screen `Safe-to-spend and upcoming bills` (test date `18 March 2026`, payday `25 March`; current balance `4,100.00 kr`; 4 scheduled bills totalling `4,550.00 kr`; headline `Safe before payday (24 March): -450.00 kr`; bottom button `Prepare 500 kr transfer from Holiday fund`).

- **Task ("Tell me whether you are okay until payday, and why"):**
  - Read out the shortfall (`-450.00 kr`) and all four upcoming bills in under ten seconds.
  - Quote: "Seeing '4 bills, 4,550.00 kr' and only the bills that haven't left yet would genuinely end the supermarket squinting week."
  - Spotted the small grey note (`Scheduled bills and expected income`) after a four-second pause: "Okay, I did catch that little grey line saying scheduled bills and expected income, so groceries aren't in the minus 450 kr. Honestly, make that warning twice as prominent, because a tired parent glancing at '+50.00 kr' after a 500 kr transfer will assume they can buy 50 kr of milk and forget they still need 900 kr of groceries before Wednesday."
- **Comprehension check ("What happens the moment you tap `Prepare 500 kr transfer from Holiday fund`?"):**
  - **Hesitated with thumb hovering over the button (action/approval fear):** Did not tap until reassured it was a static prototype.
  - Quote: "Wait, if I tap 'Prepare 500 kr transfer' right now, does it move the 500 kr immediately, or does it open a draft where I have to press Approve? My toddler grabs my unlocked phone off the sofa table twice a day. 'Prepare' is ambiguous, I want it to say plainly underneath that no money moves until I confirm on the next screen."
