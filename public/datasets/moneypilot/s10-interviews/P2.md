# Session 10 interview snapshot: P2

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. MoneyPilot is not a real product, and all participants, accounts, merchants and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P2` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Pay schedule: monthly on the last working day of the month
- Setup: main bank current account where salary lands and `AvtaleGiro` bills leave, plus a separate secondary spending card used manually during tight weeks
- Format: 30-minute video call (16 minutes story interview, 14 minutes prototype test on mobile)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 16:00)

- **Opening general claim (contradicted later):** At minute 3, `P2` said: "I am pretty on top of my balance, to be fair. I open my banking app every single morning while the coffee brews, so I always know within a hundred kroner where I stand."
- **Asked: walk me through the final seven days before your February payday (`27 February`).**
  - Paused, laughed sheepishly, and reversed the earlier claim: "Okay, when you put it like that, I check it every morning until about the 20th. Once it drops under 2,000 kr I get the morning dread and I actually stopped opening the app from the Saturday (`21 February`) until the Wednesday."
  - **What went wrong on Monday `23 February`:** `P2` had done rough mental subtraction at the bus stop on Friday `20 February` (`1,650 kr` in the account, thought all bills were done for the month). Forgot that Tellmore Mobile (`285.00 kr`, normally the 21st) and Brookline Water (`440.00 kr`, normally the 22nd) had both rolled over the weekend onto Monday `23 February`. Together with weekend groceries and transit taps (`1,239.00 kr`), the two Monday `AvtaleGiro` charges pushed the account `314.00 kr` into an unarranged overdraft for two days until a flatmate sent shared internet money on Vipps.
  - Quote: "The annoying thing is I had 900 kr sitting in an easy-access savings account I could have moved across in ten seconds if anything had reminded me those two bills were still due after the weekend."
- **Current workaround (drawer card + daily 150 kr drip):**
  - Quote: "After February, in the last week before payday I literally leave my main bank card in my bedroom drawer so a contactless tap cannot collide with a late `AvtaleGiro`. I move 150 kr a day onto my secondary card for lunch and the bus. Because if my main account says 1,800 kr on the 20th, my brain treats 1,800 kr as money I own, even when 700 kr of bills are hiding in the weeds."
- **Tangent (minutes 12 to 16, ~4 minutes):** Detailed story about fronting a `1,900 kr` birthday tapas bill for five friends three weeks ago and feeling awkward chasing one flatmate who still owed `180 kr`. Said: "Honestly if MoneyPilot had a polite automatic Vipps or chat reminder so I don't look stingy asking a friend for 180 kr back, that would save my friendships." (Did not connect to recurring bills or payday forecasting.)

## Part 2: Prototype test (16:00 to 30:00)

Shown the Level 2 static HTML discovery probe `Safe before payday and upcoming bills` in its shortfall state (test date `18 March 2026`, payday `25 March`; current balance `4,100.00 kr`; 4 scheduled bills totalling `4,550.00 kr`; headline `Safe before payday (24 March): -450.00 kr` with small grey caption `Left after scheduled bills, everyday spending not included`), followed by a secondary static wireframe mockup card showing `Prepare 500 kr transfer from Holiday fund` (non-functional wireframe; no live transfer logic built in Level 2).

- **Task ("Tell me whether you are okay until payday, and why"):**
  - Read `-450.00 kr` immediately: "No, I am 450 kr overdrawn by Tuesday the 24th because Halden Finance 1,500 kr tips me under on the day before payday."
  - Paused for eight seconds squinting at the card before speaking again: "Hold on though. Is minus 450 kr just subtracting those four bills from 4,100 kr, or has it guessed what I usually spend on food and travel? Oh, wait, 4,100 kr take away 4,550 kr is minus 450 kr, and that tiny grey line says 'Left after scheduled bills, everyday spending not included'. Make that caption much bigger. If someone is 50 kr up on bills with six days to go, they are actually 850 kr short once they eat."
- **Comprehension check ("On the static wireframe card, what would you expect to happen the moment you tapped `Prepare 500 kr transfer from Holiday fund`?"):**
  - **Refused to tap the mockup button (action/approval fear):** Held phone up and pulled thumb away from the wireframe CTA.
  - Quote: "In a real app I wouldn't press that button unless I genuinely wanted the 500 kr moved this instant. In my bank app, moving money between your own accounts doesn't ask for BankID or a second screen, one tap on a big coloured button at the bottom just executes it immediately. 'Prepare 500 kr transfer' looks way too much like 'Move 500 kr now'. If nothing moves until I approve a draft later, call it 'Preview cover transfer (won't move yet)' and show two clear steps."
