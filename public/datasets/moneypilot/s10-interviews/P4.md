# Session 10 interview snapshot: P4

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. MoneyPilot is not a real product, and all participants, accounts, merchants and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P4` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Pay schedule: monthly on the 25th
- Setup: main current account with a dedicated "Bills account" feature (`AvtaleGiro` and `eFaktura` agreements assigned to it) plus an "Emergency saver" account
- Format: 30-minute video call (18 minutes story interview, 12 minutes prototype test on mobile)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 18:00)

- **Opening general claim (contradicted by February story):** At minute 2, `P4` said: "I used to get stung in my twenties, so now I have a bulletproof system. On the 25th an automatic rule moves 11,500 kr straight into my Bills account for rent, Eastmere Municipal Fees, Nordlys Electricity and Fenwick Broadband. Whatever is left in my main balance is pure spending money, so all my bills are predictable and I never run short before payday anymore."
- **Asked: can you think back to the week of 18 to 24 February? Did you move any money between accounts at all that week?**
  - Paused, glanced at their own banking app off-camera to check February history, and laughed: "Okay, fair enough, you caught me out. On Sunday 22 February I moved 600 kr out of my Emergency saver into my main balance."
  - **Why the Bills account missed three bills:** Three recurring payments are charged to `P4`'s 16-digit debit card rather than set up as `AvtaleGiro` or `eFaktura`, so the bank's Bills account cannot ring-fence them: Pulse Gym (`299.90 kr` on the 20th), Reelbox Streaming (`109.90 kr` on the 21st) and Kestrel Car Insurance (monthly card instalment on the 19th).
  - **Annual renewal ambush:** Kestrel Car Insurance auto-renewed in February and jumped from `610.00 kr` a month to `985.00 kr` a month (`+375.00 kr`, including the first-month renewal adjustment).
  - Quote: "Because those three come off my card number instead of `AvtaleGiro`, they bypass my Bills account and hit my grocery money. And when I tried connecting a free budgeting app last year, it detected zero recurring bills on my card feed and showed an empty calendar, so I deleted it after two days."
- **Tangent (minutes 13 to 17, ~4 minutes):** Ranted about trying to cancel Pulse Gym in January and discovering a 30-day notice clause buried three menus deep on their website, then pivoted to wishing AI could negotiate bills: "Honestly if an AI agent could phone Fenwick Broadband when my 18-month promo ends and haggle 120 kr off, or cancel Pulse Gym without me sitting on hold, that would be magic."

## Part 2: Prototype test (18:00 to 30:00)

Shown the Level 2 static HTML discovery probe `Safe before payday and upcoming bills` in its shortfall state (test date `18 March 2026`, payday `25 March`; current balance `4,100.00 kr`; 4 scheduled bills totalling `4,550.00 kr`; headline `Safe before payday (24 March): -450.00 kr` with small grey caption `Left after scheduled bills, everyday spending not included`), followed by a secondary static wireframe mockup card showing `Prepare 500 kr transfer from Holiday fund` (non-functional wireframe; no live transfer logic built in Level 2).

- **Task ("Tell me whether you are okay until payday, and why"):**
  - Spotted `-450.00 kr` immediately.
  - **Observed usability friction on variable bill row:** Tapped `Nordlys Electricity 1,180.00 kr` three times expecting a drawer or history note.
  - Quote: "I kept tapping Nordlys Electricity because 1,180 kr sounds high, and my first question is 'is 1,180 kr fixed, or did it jump from 850 kr last month like my car insurance did?'"
  - Asked immediately afterwards: "Also, does this list pick up recurring debit-card subscriptions like Pulse Gym and Kestrel Car Insurance, or only `AvtaleGiro` and `eFaktura`? Because if it misses card bills, the screen will tell me I'm safe when I'm 1,400 kr short."
- **Comprehension check ("What is included in `-450.00 kr`, and what would you expect if you tapped `Prepare 500 kr transfer` on that wireframe card?"):**
  - **Misread everyday spending at first, then self-corrected by doing the sum aloud:** Started saying, "If I moved 500 kr I'd have 50 kr spare on the 24th after normal card spending... wait, let me add those up: 1,520 kr plus 1,180 kr is 2,700 kr, plus 350 kr is 3,050 kr, plus 1,500 kr is 4,550 kr. Oh! 4,100 kr minus 4,550 kr is minus 450 kr. It hasn't allowed a single krone for fuel or food over six days! That tiny grey line saying 'Left after scheduled bills, everyday spending not included' needs to sit right next to the number in bold, otherwise people will read '+50 kr' as spending money after food."
  - **Mockup button expectation:** "Prepare 500 kr transfer sounds like it stages the transfer and asks me to approve it on the next screen, which I like, as long as it never sweeps money out of my saver on its own."
