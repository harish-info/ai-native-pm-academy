# Session 10 interview snapshot: P3

Fictional practice data written for the AI-native PM academy. MoneyPilot is not a real product, and all participants, accounts, merchants and figures are invented.

## Session metadata

- Participant: `P3` (pseudonymised; real name and employer omitted)
- Consent: verbal consent recorded for written notes and pseudonymised synthesis with Claude; no audio recording made
- Pay schedule: monthly on the 25th
- Setup: main current account kept at a permanent `40,000 kr` to `45,000 kr` float, automated standing transfer on the 1st into an external `aksjesparekonto`, cashback credit card paid in full automatically every month
- Format: 30-minute video call (15 minutes story interview, 15 minutes prototype test on mobile)

## Part 1: Story interview (00:00 to 15:00)

- **Context and monthly rhythm:** Paid on the 25th. All everyday spending goes onto a rewards credit card that clears in full by `eFaktura` on the 4th; household bills (Eastmere Municipal Fees, Fenwick Broadband, Nordlys Electricity, Pulse Gym) leave the main current account throughout the month. Keeps a deliberate `42,000 kr` cash buffer in the current account at all times ("I set that float up two years ago specifically so I never have to think about what date a bill comes out").
- **How often `P3` checks the bank app:** Twice a month. Once on the 25th when the salary notification pops up, and once around the 2nd to check the `8,000 kr` `aksjesparekonto` standing transfer went through.
- **Asked: walk me through the last specific time a bill surprised you or forced you to move money before payday.**
  - Long ten-second silence.
  - Quote: "Honestly? I am struggling to think of one. Maybe four years ago when I moved flats and overlapped two municipal fee payments in the same fortnight, and even then my current account just dipped from 36,000 kr to 29,000 kr, so I didn't actually have to move anything."
- **Current workaround:** None. No notes app, no separate bills account, no spreadsheet, no pre-payday balance checks.
  - Quote: "Once Nordlys Electricity went up by 300 kr in winter, I noticed it two months later when I downloaded a PDF statement for something else."
- **Feature tangent / hypothetical wish (minutes 10 to 15, ~5 minutes):** Talked enthusiastically about wanting an AI portfolio optimiser inside their banking app:
  - Quote: "What annoys me is idle cash above my 40,000 kr buffer earning low interest. If an assistant pulled in my external aksjesparekonto, looked at my upcoming bills, and recommended which global index fund to put my spare 6,500 kr into each month, I would happily pay 50 kr a month for that."

## Part 2: Prototype test (15:00 to 30:00)

Shown the Level 2 static HTML discovery probe `Safe before payday and upcoming bills` in its shortfall state (test date `18 March 2026`, payday `25 March`; current balance `4,100.00 kr`; 4 scheduled bills totalling `4,550.00 kr`; headline `Safe before payday (24 March): -450.00 kr` with small grey caption `Left after scheduled bills, everyday spending not included`), followed by the secondary static wireframe mockup card showing `Prepare 500 kr transfer from Holiday fund`.

- **Task and spontaneous praise (polite compliment trap):**
  - Completed the scan in about twelve seconds.
  - Quote: "Oh yeah, the design looks super clean! Love the spacing and the teal accents, way nicer than my bank's cluttered insights tab. I would totally use this, I'd probably check it every week!" _(Observer note: directly contradicts Part 1 behaviour where `P3` opens their banking app twice a month and has not dropped below `29,000 kr` in four years.)_
- **Comprehension check ("What is included in `-450.00 kr`, and what would you expect if you tapped `Prepare 500 kr transfer` on the wireframe card?"):**
  - Answered both parts accurately without hesitation: "4,100 kr current balance minus 4,550 kr of scheduled bills gives minus 450 kr on 24 March, before any discretionary spending. And 'Prepare 500 kr transfer' presumably stages a 500 kr transfer from the Holiday fund for me to approve on the next step."
  - Spent the remaining six minutes suggesting visual tweaks (dark mode toggle, sparkline chart of six-month net worth, toggle for foreign currency on holiday).
